# WOIA Marketing Methodology Design

Use this skill at Task creation or when material assumptions change.

## Method

1. Clarify the business outcome, deliverable and success evidence.
2. Determine whether the request is direct-service or requires a persistent Task.
3. Select the minimum OPEA-H profile justified by ambiguity, dependencies, external effects, public exposure, spend, customer-data use and independent verification needs.
4. Select only required Marketing capabilities from registry/capabilities.json.
5. Resolve shared resource needs separately from primary Marketing providers.
6. Trigger auxiliaries only from concrete evidence.
7. Record expected outputs, evidence, effect classes and Human Review boundaries in the Task/TaskCell.

## Capability selection guidance

- marketing-strategy: campaign/business objective, offer, positioning, channel mix, KPI plan or strategic tradeoffs.
- audience: audience/segment research, targeting assumptions, customer/lead segment definition.
- content-copy: channel-ready copy/content adaptation.
- creative: creative brief, asset requirements, creative QA and asset handoff.
- channel-execution: authorized publication/activation/distribution or external marketing actions.
- analytics: measurement plan, performance analysis, attribution limitations, learnings.

Do not select a provider merely because it is installed.

## OPEA-H guidance

- Internal low-risk draft with clear scope: task-execution.
- Strategy/campaign design: planned-execution.
- Public campaign/paid activation/external customer communication: audited-execution or opea-h-full depending planning complexity.
- Complex campaign with multiple channels, spend or irreversible effects: opea-h-full.

Human Review is not automatically required for every internal artifact, but it is required when policy/effect authority or the selected profile says so.
